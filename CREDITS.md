# Credits

Arcade Foundry: Insert Coin brings Sega's arcade games to your own devices, from the ROM sets you own. It's made by one developer, standing on the work of many people. Thank you all.

Apart from its creator, the people and projects named here are credited for their work, which the app uses under their licences. They are not involved with Arcade Foundry: Insert Coin and have not endorsed it.

## Arcade Foundry: Insert Coin

- **TheBrinkOfTomorrow**: creator of Arcade Foundry: Insert Coin and its game cores.

## Where the cores began

- **Ben Templeman (alphanu1)**: the [Daytona USA static recompilation](https://github.com/alphanu1/daytona-arcade-recomp) that the Model 1 and Model 2 cores grew from (BSD 3-Clause).

## Model 1 and Model 2 cores

Parts of these cores come from [MAME](https://www.mamedev.org) (BSD 3-Clause). Its authors include:

- **Farfetch'd and R. Belmont**: NEC V60 processor
- **Ville Linde**: Analog Devices SHARC processor
- **Angelo Salese, ElSemi and Matthew Daniels**: Fujitsu MB86235 processor
- **Andreas Naive, Olivier Galibert and David Haywood**: Sega 315-5881 protection
- **Olivier Galibert**: Model 1 geometry processor, Model 1 video and MPEG sound
- **Ariane Fugmann**: Model 1 and Model 2 link boards
- **Miguel Angel Horna**: MultiPCM sound
- **ElSemi and R. Belmont, with thanks to kingshriek**: SCSP sound
- **R. Belmont and Olivier Galibert**: Digital Sound Board
- **Dirk Best, AJR, Aaron Giles and Wilbert Pol**: I/O boards

They also use:

- **[ymfm](https://github.com/aaronsgiles/ymfm)** by Aaron Giles: Yamaha FM sound (BSD 3-Clause)
- **[Berkeley SoftFloat](http://www.jhauser.us/arithmetic/SoftFloat.html)** by John R. Hauser: floating point (BSD 3-Clause)
- **[7-Zip LZMA SDK](https://www.7-zip.org/sdk.html)** by Igor Pavlov: reading .7z ROM sets (public domain)

## Model 3 cores

- **[Supermodel](https://github.com/trzy/Supermodel)** by The Supermodel Team: the Model 3 cores are built from it (GPL 3.0 or later). Supermodel carries its own credits for the code it uses.

## Lindbergh cores

- **[gl4es](https://github.com/ptitSeb/gl4es)** by ptitSeb: OpenGL on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV (MIT)
- **DejaVu Sans Mono**: the boot screen's font (Bitstream Vera and DejaVu licence)
- **[OpenGL Registry](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/OpenGL-Registry)** by the Khronos Group: OpenGL definitions, used when building (Apache 2.0)

## The app

- **[SDL](https://www.libsdl.org)** by Sam Lantinga and the SDL contributors: the Linux app, and the cores on Linux (zlib)
