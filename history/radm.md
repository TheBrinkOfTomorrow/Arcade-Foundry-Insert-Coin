# Rad Mobile

Sega · 1990 · Sega System 32

Rad Mobile is a driving game in which you race a sports car across the United States, from Los Angeles to New York, against the clock and a field of rival cars. Each stage brings its own weather and time of day, and you switch on the headlights at night and the wipers in the rain. Sonic the Hedgehog makes an early appearance as a doll hanging from the rear-view mirror.

It was one of the first games for the System 32, Sega's 32-bit board with a V60 CPU, which later ran Rad Rally, Golden Axe: The Revenge of Death Adder and SegaSonic the Hedgehog.
