# G-LOC Air Battle

Sega · 1990 · Sega Y Board

G-LOC: Air Battle is a jet fighter shooter that follows After Burner, seen from inside the cockpit for most of the game. The player flies a series of missions, each with a set number of enemy aircraft and ground targets to destroy before time runs out, using the vulcan cannon, homing missiles and an after burner to escape enemies on the player's tail. The title comes from "G-induced loss of consciousness", the blackout a pilot suffers in hard turns.

It ran on Sega's Y Board, which drives its sprites with three 68000 processors and can rotate the whole sprite layer. Sega also built it into the R360, a cockpit that could spin the player through a full circle on two axes.
