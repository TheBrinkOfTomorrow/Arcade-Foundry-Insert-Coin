# Magical Truck Adventure

Sega · 1998 · Sega Model 3

Magical Truck Adventure is a two-player race on a railway handcar. Two children, Roy and Alma, ride a cart along the rails, and the cabinet has a real pumping lever: the harder the two players pump it up and down together, the faster the cart goes. Foot pedals make the riders dodge and jump obstacles.

The track runs through stranger and stranger stages, and it only goes fast when both players pump together. It was released in Japan in 1998.

It stayed an arcade-only curiosity until 2026, when it came home inside Yakuza Kiwami 3 & Dark Ties, where one player can pump with both sticks of a controller. In this app the stick's up and down pump the lever.
