# INDY 500

Sega AM1 · 1995 · Sega Model 2B

INDY 500 is Sega's officially licensed IndyCar game, made by the AM1 team under Rikiya Nakagawa. It was AM1's second 3D game after Wing War, and it was first planned for the Model 3 board, until delays to that hardware moved it to Model 2.

There are three courses: the Indianapolis Motor Speedway itself and two fictional ones, Highland Raceway and Bayside Street. The cars reach about 380 km/h, more in another car's slipstream. Races start rolling behind the pace car, and a Long Race mode runs 16 to 20 laps instead of the usual three. The wheel has force feedback, and up to eight cabinets can be linked.

A Saturn version was announced but cancelled, and its team moved on to Daytona USA Championship Circuit Edition, so the game stayed in arcades. This app plays the Twin cabinet version, Revision A.
