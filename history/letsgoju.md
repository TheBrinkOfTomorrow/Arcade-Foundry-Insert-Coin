# Let's Go Jungle!

Sega AM3 · 2006 · Sega Lindbergh

Let's Go Jungle!: Lost on the Island of Spice is a light-hearted light-gun game for two, made by Sega's AM3 team. Two tourists, a man and a woman, are stranded on an island overrun by giant insects and creatures, and they shoot their way out together with guns mounted on the cabinet.

There are four stages, a jungle safari, a river cruise, an elephant ride and island hopping, each ending with a giant boss: a spider, a frog, a mutated plant and a huge butterfly. Along the way, short team-up moments ask both players to duck, start an engine or jump at the same time, and every stage ends with a heart-shaped score for how well the pair worked together.

A larger "Special" cabinet version also appeared, and the idea returned in Let's Go Island. The game never came to home consoles.
