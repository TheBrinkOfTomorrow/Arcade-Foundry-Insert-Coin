# Super Monaco GP

Sega · 1989 · Sega X Board

Super Monaco GP is a Formula One racing game set on the streets of Monaco. You pick an automatic, four-speed or seven-speed gearbox, set a time on a qualifying lap to earn your place on the grid, then race against the field, staying above the position limit on every lap to keep going.

It ran on the X Board, the hardware behind After Burner and Thunder Blade, with a second sound board of its own. The deluxe and cockpit cabinets added a moving "Air Drive" seat. Its main program is protected by a Hitachi FD1094 encrypted CPU.
