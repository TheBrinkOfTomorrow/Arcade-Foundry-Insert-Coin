# Spikeout

Amusement Vision · 1998 · Sega Model 3

Spikeout is a 3D brawler built for teams: up to four linked cabinets put four players in the same fight, working their way through a city's gangs together. It was directed and produced by Toshihiro Nagoshi, the director of Daytona USA, at his Sega studio Amusement Vision.

You choose one of four fighters, Spike, Linda, Tony and Jeanne, and fight through a free-roaming 3D city, building combos and charging up special attacks against crowds of enemies and big bosses.

It was one of Japan's most successful arcade games in late 1998, and Spikeout Final Edition followed in 1999. Nagoshi said the Dreamcast was not powerful enough for it, so it stayed in arcades, apart from an Xbox follow-up in 2005, until Final Edition came home inside Sega's Like a Dragon games. This app has both Spikeout and Final Edition.
