# Gunblade NY

Sega AM3 · 1995 · Sega Model 2B

Gunblade NY puts you in the door of an attack helicopter over New York, manning a heavy machine gun. Robotic terrorists have taken the city during a session of the UN General Assembly, and a single helicopter is sent to win it back. Sega's AM3 team made it, and the gun is a large mounted turret, so ammunition never runs out.

You don't fly the helicopter; you shoot. It flies over Times Square, the UN headquarters, the Brooklyn Bridge and Battery Park, through two campaigns of four levels with a boss at the end of most of them. You can shoot down incoming rockets, and three hits end your turn. Destroying a missile launcher at Battery Park decides between the good and bad endings. A second player can join as another gunner.

It was the most successful dedicated arcade game in Japan in June 1996. The sequel, L.A. Machineguns, followed on Model 3 in 1998 (also in this app), and both came to the Wii in 2010 in Sega Arcade Hits Pack. This app plays Revision A.
