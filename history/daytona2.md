# Daytona USA 2

Sega AM2 · 1998 · Sega Model 3

Daytona USA 2: Battle on the Edge is the Model 3 sequel to Daytona USA, made by AM2 with Toshihiro Nagoshi producing and Makoto Osaki directing. Instead of one car you now choose a car and driver from several, each handling differently, and up to sixteen cabinets can be linked.

There are three courses: Forest Dome for beginners, the Joypolis amusement park for advanced players and the long Virtua City for experts. The music is heavier rock than the first game's, with vocals by Dennis St. James and guitar from Winger's Reb Beach; operators can switch to an alternative soundtrack sung by Takenobu Mitsuyoshi, the voice of the original.

Power Edition followed at the end of 1998 with revised handling, tougher opponents, a reworked beginner course and a Challenge race through all three tracks. The game stayed in arcades for 25 years, until Power Edition appeared in Like a Dragon Gaiden in 2023. This app has both editions.
