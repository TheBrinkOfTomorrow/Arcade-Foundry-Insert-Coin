# F1 Exhaust Note

Sega · 1991 · Sega System 32

F1 Exhaust Note is a Formula One racing game. You pick an automatic or a manual gearbox, then race a field of cars over four laps of a grand prix circuit, collecting points for your finishing position before the clock runs out. The sit-down cabinet seats two drivers side by side who race each other in the same event.

Each seat has its own System 32 board, and the two boards share a small block of memory so the cars on both screens race in one field. That is why both boards run here even when only one seat is driven. Its sequel, F1 Super Lap, followed in 1993.
