# Space Harrier

Sega · 1985 · Sega Hang-On board

Space Harrier is Sega's 1985 third-person shooter, designed by Yu Suzuki. You run and fly through the Fantasy Zone, a checkerboard world of pillars, trees and dragons, shooting everything that comes at you over eighteen stages, most of them ending with a boss.

It ran on the sprite-scaling board built for Hang-On, controlled with an analog flight stick. The deluxe sit-down cabinet moved with the stick, tilting the player's seat as the Harrier flew.

It was ported to many home systems, among them the Sega Master System, the 32X and the Sega Saturn.
