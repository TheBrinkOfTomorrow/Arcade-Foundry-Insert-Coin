# Top Skater

Sega AM3 · 1997 · Sega Model 2C

Top Skater is played standing up: you ride a skateboard platform that swings and tilts under your feet, and lean to steer the skater on screen. Sega's AM3 team made it, directed by Kenji Kanno, and AM4 helped design the controller and cabinet.

You skate downhill courses full of ramps and rails, scoring points for tricks, with a timer you extend by passing through bonus rings or landing certain tricks. The soundtrack is ten punk songs by Pennywise.

Kenji Kanno went on to create Crazy Taxi, which shares Top Skater's look and its taste for punk rock, and Neversoft studied the game while making Tony Hawk's Pro Skater. Sega followed it with Air Trix in 2001 and reused the idea in Ollie King. It never came to home consoles. This app plays the Export version, Revision A.
