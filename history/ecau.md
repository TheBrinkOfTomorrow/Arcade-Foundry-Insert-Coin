# Emergency Call Ambulance

Sega · 1999 · Sega Model 3

Emergency Call Ambulance makes you a Chicago Fire Department paramedic racing an ambulance through the city. Instead of beating other cars to the finish, you have to reach each emergency and get the patient to hospital in time.

There are four calls: a child hurt in a car crash, a police officer who has been shot, a pregnant woman caught in a hotel fire and the President, whose plane has come down in Lake Michigan. The cabinet has a wheel, a gear stick and pedals, with a choice of automatic or manual gears.

A Dreamcast collection with Brave Firefighters and Jambo! Safari was reported but never released, so for years the game stayed in arcades. It came home in 2026, inside Yakuza Kiwami 3 & Dark Ties.
