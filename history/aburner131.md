# After Burner

Sega · 1987 · Sega X Board

After Burner is a jet-fighter shooting game from Yu Suzuki's team. You fly an F-14 Tomcat through 18 stages of enemy jets, firing the vulcan cannon and lock-on missiles, rolling to dodge incoming fire and refuelling in mid-air between stages.

It came as an upright cabinet and as a cockpit that rolled and pitched with the plane. After Burner II followed later the same year and added a throttle lever. This is version 1.31 of the original.

It was ported to the Master System, the Sharp X68000 and many home computers.
