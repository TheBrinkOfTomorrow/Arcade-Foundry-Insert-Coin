# Over Rev

Jaleco · 1997 · Sega Model 2B

Over Rev was Jaleco's second racing game for Sega's Model 2 board, after Super GT 24h. It is a bright, fast street racer set around Japan.

There are four courses: the streets of Shibuya, Ariake, where you drive under Tokyo Big Sight, the Tsukuba circuit and the forest roads of Hakone, past waterfalls. Seven cars are in the game, three of them hidden, including a pick-up truck. Challenge Cup takes you through the courses in order, needing a better finishing place each time; Time Attack is a race against the clock on any course.

Over Rev never came to home consoles.
