# Pilot Kids

Psikyo · 1998 · Sega Model 2B

Pilot Kids is Psikyo's second shooting game for Sega's Model 2 board, after Zero Gunner, and a much lighter one. It is a "cute 'em up": a toy-sized pilot flies a radio-controlled plane through a child's playroom, battling toy enemies.

It scrolls sideways, like Zero Gunner, and fills the screen with bullets in the Psikyo tradition. Two players can play together.

It was a late, rare Model 2 game and never came to home consoles. This app plays Revision A.
