# Sega Touring Car Championship

Sega AM Annex · 1996 · Sega Model 2C

Sega Touring Car Championship comes from the team behind Sega Rally: a small group called AM Annex, produced by Tetsuya Mizuguchi, with Kenji Sasaki directing and Sohei Yamamoto programming.

The cars are licensed from the German touring car championship, the DTM, of 1995 and 1996, raced flat out on three fictional circuits designed like real ones: Country Circuit, Grünwalt Circuit and Brickwall Town, with a fourth, secret course for those who reach the end of the season. It is fast and demanding, and critics praised its speed and realism even as they found it hard.

It came to the Saturn in 1997, with a car-tuning mode and the two Sega Rally cars as extras, and to Windows in 1998.
