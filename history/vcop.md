# Virtua Cop

Sega AM2 · 1994 · Sega Model 2

Virtua Cop brought 3D polygons to the light-gun game. Until then, gun games used flat photos or sprites; in Virtua Cop the criminals are 3D models that react to where they are hit, and the camera swoops through the streets, warehouses and offices with each wave. Sega's AM2 team made it, supervised by Yu Suzuki.

You play police officers Michael "Rage" Hardy and James "Smarty" Cools against the E.V.I.L. syndicate. Coloured circles shrink around each criminal to show who is about to fire. Shooting a civilian costs a life, and a "justice shot" to the gun hand earns extra points. It was also one of the first games where you could shoot through glass.

It was Japan's highest-earning dedicated arcade game of 1995. It came to the Saturn with the Virtua Gun in 1995 and to Windows and the PlayStation 2 later, and it influenced Time Crisis, The House of the Dead and GoldenEye 007. This app plays Revision B.
