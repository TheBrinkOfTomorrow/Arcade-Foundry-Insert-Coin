# Dead or Alive

Tecmo (Team Ninja) · 1996 · Sega Model 2B

Dead or Alive was Tecmo's first fighting game, led by Tomonobu Itagaki and built on Sega's Model 2 board, the same hardware as Virtua Fighter 2. It plays faster than Virtua Fighter, with simpler commands, and it replaced the guard button with a hold: catch an attacking limb at the right moment and you turn the attack back on its owner. It was one of the first fighting games to give each kind of attack its own counter.

The edges of each arena are Danger Zones that explode when a fighter lands on them, causing heavy damage and launching them for more hits. The original roster has nine fighters, among them Kasumi, Ryu Hayabusa from Ninja Gaiden, Tina Armstrong, Jann Lee, Leifang and Zack.

The game helped return Tecmo to profit in 1996. It came to the Saturn in Japan in 1997 and to the PlayStation in 1998, and it started a long series beginning with Dead or Alive 2 in 1999. This app plays Revision B.
