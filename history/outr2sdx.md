# OutRun 2 SP SDX

Sega AM2 · 2006 · Sega Lindbergh

OutRun 2 brought back Sega's 1986 classic: a Ferrari, a passenger beside you and a road that forks again and again, so each run passes through 5 of 15 stages. Sega AM2 made it with Yu Suzuki producing, and it was the first OutRun game in ten years. Sega licensed seven Ferraris for it, including the original game's Testarossa.

OutRun 2 SP added 15 new courses, and SP SDX moved the game to the Lindbergh board in 2006 with a wider picture. Alongside the classic race against the clock is Heart Attack mode, where your passenger asks for stunts such as drifting, overtaking and knocking over cones, and a good enough run earns a more romantic ending. The music is newly arranged from the 1986 soundtrack.

At home it became OutRun 2 on the Xbox in 2004 and OutRun 2006: Coast 2 Coast, which includes the SP courses.
