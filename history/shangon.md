# Super Hang-On

Sega · 1987 · Sega Out Run board

Super Hang-On is the follow-up to Sega's 1985 motorbike game Hang-On, built on the same board as Out Run. You race a superbike against the clock past other riders, steering by leaning, and at top speed a turbo button pushes the bike faster still.

You pick one of four courses named after continents: Africa for beginners, then Asia, America and Europe, each longer than the last. The ride-on cabinet had you lean a full-size bike to steer; sit-down and upright cabinets used handlebars instead.

It was ported to the Mega Drive and many home computers.
