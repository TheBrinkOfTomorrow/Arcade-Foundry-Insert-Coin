# Sega Ski Super G

Sega · 1996 · Sega Model 2C

Sega Ski Super G is a downhill skiing race in which you stand on the cabinet's skis and work them with your legs. The foot pedals are slanted so you lean forward into the run like a real skier, which makes it one of Sega's more physical games.

You race against other skiers and the clock in World Series mode, or chase your own best time in Time Attack. Different kinds of snow change how the skis grip and slide. Its music is by Masanori Takeuchi and David Leytze; the attract-mode song came back as "Shine on the Top" in Harley-Davidson & L.A. Riders.

The game had a limited release and is one of the rarest Model 2 games. Sega followed it with Ski Champ in 1998, and it never came to home consoles.
