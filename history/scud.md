# Scud Race

Sega AM2 · 1996 · Sega Model 3

Scud Race, called Sega Super GT in North America, was the first racing game on Sega's Model 3 board. Toshihiro Nagoshi, the director of Daytona USA, directed and produced it, with Yu Suzuki advising on how the cars drift and sound, and AM4 designed the cabinet.

It is based on the BPR Global GT Series of the mid-1990s, with GT cars from four of the 1996 season's teams. There are four courses, two of them for beginners, by day and by night. The American name changed because "Scud" recalled the missiles of the Gulf War.

Scud Race Plus followed in Japan in 1997 with mirrored versions of every course and a beginner course set in a giant playhouse, where you can also race as a cat, a tank or an AM2 crew bus. A Dreamcast version was shown but cancelled; the four courses later appeared as an extra in OutRun 2 on the Xbox. This app has both Scud Race and Scud Race Plus.
