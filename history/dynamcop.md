# Dynamite Cop

Sega AM1 · 1998 · Sega Model 2A

Dynamite Cop, Dynamite Deka 2 in Japan, is the sequel to Die Hard Arcade, a 3D brawler for one or two players made by Sega's AM1 team. Pirates led by Wolf "White Fang" Hongo, the villain of the first game, have taken the President's daughter hostage on a cruise ship, and you fight your way through the ship and onto a deserted island to rescue her.

You play as Bruno Delinger, Jean Ivy or Eddie Brown. As in Die Hard Arcade, almost anything lying around can be picked up and used as a weapon, and short button-press scenes decide how you deal with each new threat. A roast chicken leg from Golden Axe is hidden on the island.

It came to the Dreamcast in 1999, with the 1980 Sega game Tranquilizer Gun as an unlockable extra. This app plays the Export version.
