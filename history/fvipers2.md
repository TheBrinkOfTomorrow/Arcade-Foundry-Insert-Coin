# Fighting Vipers 2

Sega AM2 · 1998 · Sega Model 3

Fighting Vipers 2 brings AM2's armoured street fighters to the Model 3 board. The walled arenas and breakable armour of the first game return, and a new Super K.O. ends a round at once with a precisely timed combo.

Alongside the original cast are four newcomers: Emi, a schoolgirl fighting in armour she built herself; Charlie, a BMX rider and Picky's rival; and two hidden fighters, Del Sol, a masked wrestler, and Kuhn, who copies everyone else's moves.

It came to the Dreamcast in Japan and Europe in 2001, and decades later appeared as a playable arcade machine in Like a Dragon Gaiden.
