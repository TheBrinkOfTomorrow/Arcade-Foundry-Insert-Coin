# Le Mans 24

Sega · 1997 · Sega Model 3

Le Mans 24 is Sega's officially licensed take on the 24 Hours of Le Mans, approved by the race's organiser, the Automobile Club de l'Ouest. It ran on the Model 3 board, and some of its cars carried over from Scud Race.

The field is built around the 1996 race, with the McLaren F1 GTR, the Nissan Skyline GT-R GT1, the Porsche 911 GT1 and a Ferrari F40, joined by famous Le Mans winners of the past: the Sauber Mercedes C9 of 1989 and the Mazda 787B of 1991. A cheat code unlocks the Gulf Porsche 917 of 1971, and another lets Sonic the Hedgehog join the race.

It was the third most popular dedicated arcade game in Japan at the end of 1997, and it never came to home consoles.
