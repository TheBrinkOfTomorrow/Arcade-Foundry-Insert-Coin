# Virtua Fighter 2

Sega AM2 · 1994 · Sega Model 2

Virtua Fighter 2 is the game the Model 2 board was built for. Yu Suzuki's AM2 team wanted texture-mapped fighters, which the Model 1 board could not draw, so Sega adapted an expensive texture-mapping chip from Lockheed Martin into a cheaper one for the new board. The result ran at 60 frames a second, twice the rate of the first game, and was one of the first games animated with motion capture.

All of the first game's fighters return, with around 500 new moves between them, and two newcomers: Shun Di, a drunken-fist master, and Lion Rafale, a French mantis-style fighter.

It was Japan's highest-earning arcade game in both 1995 and 1996 and one of Sega's best-selling arcade games. The Saturn version sold over two million copies, and later versions appeared on the Mega Drive, Windows, PlayStation 2, iOS, PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360. Virtua Fighter 3 followed on Model 3 in 1996. This app plays version 2.1.
