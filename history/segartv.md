# Sega Race TV

Sega AM Plus · 2008 · Sega Lindbergh

Sega Race TV is a street racing game designed by Yu Suzuki, the creator of Out Run, Virtua Racing and Virtua Fighter. It was made by his team, AM Plus, after he left AM2, and it was his last game with Sega.

Its cars are American muscle cars and roadsters, among them the Ford Mustang GT, the Chevrolet Camaro concept, a Corvette C3 Stingray, the Plymouth HEMI Cuda and Prowler, the Mazda MX-5 and the Ruf RK Roadster, each with a convertible version. Customised cars can be unlocked with passwords entered at a cabinet.

It was released in Japan in 2008 and in North America and Europe later, and it never came to home consoles.
