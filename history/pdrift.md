# Power Drift

Sega · 1988 · Sega Y Board

Power Drift is a buggy racing game on twisting, rollercoaster-like tracks that climb, drop and loop over one another. The player picks one of twelve drivers and races eleven rivals over five-lap circuits, finishing in the top three to go on to the next course.

It ran on Sega's Y Board, which uses three 68000 processors and can rotate the whole sprite layer, giving the tracks their tilt and sway; the deluxe cabinet's seat moved with the car.
