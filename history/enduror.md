# Enduro Racer

Sega · 1986 · Sega Hang-On board

Enduro Racer is Sega's 1986 off-road motorbike racer. You ride a dirt bike over stages of dirt tracks, jumps and rough ground, passing other riders and reaching each checkpoint before time runs out. Pulling back on the handlebars lifts the front wheel for a wheelie, which carries the bike over bumps and launches it off ramps.

The cabinet had motocross handlebars with a twist grip throttle and a brake lever, and you pulled the bars up for wheelies. It runs on the same board as Hang-On and Space Harrier, with an encrypted main CPU.

It was ported to the Sega Master System as Enduro Racer, seen from an isometric view.
