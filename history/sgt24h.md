# Super GT 24h

Jaleco · 1996 · Sega Model 2B

Super GT 24h was made for Sega's Model 2 board by Jaleco, with Sega's support. It is a GT racer with fictional cars styled after the real GT machines of the mid-1990s.

Its signature idea is the 24-hour race squeezed into a few minutes: on the longest course, the time of day changes as you complete each section, so you race from daylight through dusk into night, as in a real endurance race.

The game was rare even when new and is rarer now. A much weaker Saturn version came out as GT24. It may also have inspired Sega's own endurance racer, Le Mans 24, on Model 3 in 1997.
