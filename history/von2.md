# Cyber Troopers Virtual-On Oratorio Tangram

Sega AM3 · 1998 · Sega Model 3

Oratorio Tangram is the sequel to Cyber Troopers Virtual-On, again played with two joysticks. It is set twenty years after the first game: a rogue program called Tangram has taken over every Virtuaroid but yours, and you fight your way through them to face Tangram itself.

Hajime Katoki designed the new Virtuaroids. Temjin and Raiden return, joined by Apharmd, Fei-Yen, Grys-Vok and Cypher among others. There are two endings, and a time limit for the whole run.

A later edition moved to Sega's NAOMI board. The game came to the Dreamcast in 1999, to the Xbox 360 in 2009 and to the PlayStation 4 in Japan in 2019.
