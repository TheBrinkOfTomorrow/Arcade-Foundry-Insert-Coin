# Dynamite Baseball 97

Sega · 1997 · Sega Model 2B

Dynamite Baseball 97 is Sega's 3D arcade baseball game for Japan, the follow-up to Dynamite Baseball of 1996. It was released in Japanese arcades in April 1997, and two players can play against each other.

The game is quick to pick up: a stick and a few buttons cover pitching, batting and fielding, and the 3D camera follows the ball through the stadium. Its music is by Masanori Takeuchi.

The series carried on with Dynamite Baseball NAOMI in 1998 and Dynamite Baseball '99, and a Korean version of this game was released as Hanguk Pro Yagu 98. It never came to home consoles.
