# Star Wars Arcade

Sega AM3 · 1993 · Sega Model 1

Star Wars Arcade puts you in the cockpit of an X-wing or a Y-wing for the battles of the original film trilogy. Sega's AM3 team built it on the Model 1 board, the hardware of Virtua Racing and Virtua Fighter.

Two players can share a ship, one flying and one on the guns. The missions take you from TIE fighter attacks in an asteroid field to an attack on a Super Star Destroyer and finally the assault on the Death Star, viewed from the cockpit or from behind your ship.

A home version of the same name was a launch game for the Sega 32X in 1994. Sega returned to the films with Star Wars Trilogy Arcade on Model 3 in 1998, also in this app.
