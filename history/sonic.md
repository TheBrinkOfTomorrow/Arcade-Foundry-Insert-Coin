# SegaSonic the Hedgehog

Sega · 1992 · Sega System 32

SegaSonic the Hedgehog is an isometric arcade game in which Sonic, Mighty the Armadillo and Ray the Flying Squirrel, captured by Dr. Robotnik and left on his island, race to escape it. Up to three players each roll a trackball to run and press one button to spin jump, dodging traps, lava and collapsing floors on the way off the island.

It was released only in Japan and marks the first appearance of Mighty and Ray. The cabinet has a trackball for each of the three players; here the stick or d-pad rolls the trackball, and up to two players can play.
