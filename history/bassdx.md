# Sega Bass Fishing

Sega AM1 · 1998 · Sega Model 3

Sega Bass Fishing is the export name of Get Bass, Sega's arcade fishing game, and its cabinet is its big idea: a rod with a working reel, its line running into the machine, which pulls back as the fish fights. You cast with the stick, work the lure and reel in, feeling for when to give line and when to take it.

There are four stages at different times of day. You must catch a target weight of bass before the clock runs out to move on, and the last stage allows only one catch, from the biggest and hardest fish in the game. Each catch is weighed and ranked, from Small One to Huge.

It was one of Japan's top arcade games in early 1998. The Dreamcast version of 1999 added four stages and sold well, and it later came to Windows, the Wii, the Xbox 360, PlayStation 3 and Steam. Sequels include Sega Bass Fishing 2 and Sega Bass Fishing Challenge. This app plays the Deluxe cabinet's US version; the Japanese versions are under Get Bass.
