# GP Rider

Sega · 1990 · Sega X Board

GP Rider is a motorcycle Grand Prix racing game played on a ride-on bike: you lean to steer, twist the grip for throttle and shift gears with your foot. You race a field of riders over four laps against the clock, passing checkpoints for more time. Two cabinets could be linked so that two players race each other.

It ran on the X Board, the hardware behind After Burner and Super Monaco GP. Its main program is protected by a Hitachi FD1094 encrypted CPU.
