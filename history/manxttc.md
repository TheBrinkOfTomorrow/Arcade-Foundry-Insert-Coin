# Manx TT Superbike

Sega AM3 and AM4 · 1995 · Sega Model 2

Manx TT Superbike puts you on a full-size motorcycle that tilts as you shift your weight, a descendant of Sega's own Hang-On cabinet of 1985 but far more sensitive. Produced by Tetsuya Mizuguchi, who also produced Sega Rally, it is set on the Isle of Man, home of the famous Tourist Trophy road races.

There are two courses: Laxey Coast, a fictional beginner's course through Isle of Man scenery, and the TT Course, a harder course based on the real circuit. The bikes are modelled on Honda's RC45 superbike, and Honda and Castrol advised on how the engines should sound. Up to eight cabinets can be linked for one race.

Tantalus brought it to the Saturn in 1997, with a two-player split screen, and a PC version followed the same year. Sega later reused the Manx TT cabinet for Motor Raid, sold as a conversion kit in 1997. This app plays the Twin cabinet version, Revision C.
