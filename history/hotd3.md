# The House of the Dead III

Wow Entertainment · 2002 · Sega Chihiro

The House of the Dead III is the third game in Sega's zombie light-gun series. Wow Entertainment, the former AM1 team, made it, and Takashi Oda, who directed the first game, directed it again. It runs on the Chihiro board, which is built from the same parts as the original Xbox.

The story jumps to 2019, years after the first game's mansion, when a worldwide outbreak has left civilization in ruins. Thomas Rogan, the hero of the first game, leads a raid on a research facility and goes missing. His daughter Lisa and his old partner G go in after him.

The pistols of the earlier games are now shotguns. On the cabinet, you reload by pumping the gun. Before most chapters the players pick which route to take next. The areas are the same either way, but the order changes what happens, and there are four endings. Bosses can be stopped mid-attack by shooting their weak points fast enough.

The game came to the Xbox in 2002 with The House of the Dead 2 as a bonus, then to Windows, to the Wii in a pairing with The House of the Dead 2, and to the PlayStation 3 in 2012 with PlayStation Move support.
