# Sega Water Ski

Sega AM1 · 1997 · Sega Model 2C

Sega Water Ski puts you on a pair of foot pads: slide them left and right to steer behind the tow boat, and lift your heels to jump. Sega's AM1 team made it, following the stand-up controls of Top Skater and Sega Ski Super G.

The game is all about stunts. You weave across the boat's wake and launch off ramps to perform tricks for points before the timer runs out. It is a single-player game.

It was released in Japan at the end of 1997 and in the West early in 1998, and it never came to home consoles. This app plays the Japanese version, Revision A.
