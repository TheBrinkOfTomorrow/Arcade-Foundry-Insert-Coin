# Dirt Devils

Sega · 1998 · Sega Model 3

Dirt Devils is an off-road race for all-terrain vehicles, released in Japan in October 1998 on a late version of the Model 3 board.

You choose between a Humvee, a sand buggy and a Beetle-based buggy and race on three courses, with a fourth hidden inside a huge dome arena.

It is one of the lesser-known Model 3 games and never came to home consoles.
