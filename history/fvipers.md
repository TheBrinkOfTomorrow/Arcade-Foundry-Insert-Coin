# Fighting Vipers

Sega AM2 · 1995 · Sega Model 2

Fighting Vipers is AM2's rougher, louder companion to Virtua Fighter 2, built on the same engine and produced by Yu Suzuki. Its fighters are street kids and rockers from the fictional Armstone City, and its arenas are closed in by walls and fences, so there are no ring-outs: instead, you can bounce opponents off the walls or smash them clean through.

Each fighter wears armour, and a heavy enough hit breaks it away, leaving them open to more damage. The roster includes Bahn, Grace, Raxel, Tokio, Sanman, Jane, Candy and Picky, with Mahler as a hidden fighter, and B.M., the city's mayor, as the final opponent.

It was a hit in Japan and came to the Saturn in 1996. Its cast joined Virtua Fighter's in Fighters Megamix, Fighting Vipers 2 followed on Model 3 in 1998, and a Sonic model hidden in the game led to Sonic the Fighters. This app plays Revision D.
