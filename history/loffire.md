# Line of Fire

Sega · 1989 · Sega X Board

Line of Fire is a two-player shooting game played with machine guns mounted on the cabinet. Two soldiers have stolen a secret machine gun from a guerrilla base and must fight their way out through the jungle, a harbour and enemy bases, firing at soldiers, vehicles and helicopters, with a limited supply of missiles for the heavier targets.

It ran on the X Board, the hardware behind After Burner and Thunder Blade. Its main program is protected by a Hitachi FD1094 encrypted CPU.
