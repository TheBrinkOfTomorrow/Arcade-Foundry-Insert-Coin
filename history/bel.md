# Behind Enemy Lines

Real 3D and Sega · 1997 · Sega Model 2C

Behind Enemy Lines is a first-person shooting game in which you charge through enemy territory, destroying everything in your path. It was developed by Real 3D, the Lockheed Martin division behind the 3D graphics chips of Sega's Model 2 and Model 3 boards, and published by Sega in Japan in December 1997.

Two players can play together. Each aims at the screen and fires through waves of soldiers, vehicles and emplacements, in the style of Sega's gun games of the time.

It is one of the rarer Model 2 games and never came to home consoles.
