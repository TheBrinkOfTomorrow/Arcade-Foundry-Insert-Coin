# After Burner II

Sega · 1987 · Sega X Board

After Burner II is a jet-fighter shooting game from Yu Suzuki's team, released the same year as the original After Burner. You fly an F-14 through stage after stage of enemy planes, firing the vulcan cannon and lock-on missiles, rolling to dodge and refuelling in the air between stages.

The sequel added a throttle lever, so you can speed up or slow down, along with new stages. It came in an upright cabinet, a sit-down cockpit, and a deluxe cockpit that rolled and tilted with the plane.

It was ported to the Mega Drive, the PC Engine and many home computers.
