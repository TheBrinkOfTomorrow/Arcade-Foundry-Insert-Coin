# OutRunners

Sega · 1992 · Sega System Multi 32

OutRunners is the arcade sequel to Out Run, made for Sega's two-screen System Multi 32 board so two players could race side by side. You pick one of eight cars, each with its own handling, and race a rival around the world through stages that branch, as in the original.

The car radio returns with new arrangements of Out Run's music and new tracks, chosen with buttons on the dashboard while you drive.

It was ported to the Mega Drive in 1994.
