# Virtua Striker

Sega AM2 · 1994 · Sega Model 2B

Virtua Striker was the first football game drawn in 3D, and Sega advertised it as exactly that. Yu Suzuki proposed it to AM2's Satoshi Mifune, who was unsure at first that football would work in 3D, and the texture-mapped players of the Model 2 board made it look unlike any football game before it.

It uses three buttons: one for short passes and sliding tackles, one for long balls, which cross automatically near the box, and one for shots, held for power or tapped for headers and volleys. Each team plays a fixed formation, and two players can play against each other.

It was the second most successful arcade game in Japan in July 1995. It started a series that ran through Virtua Striker 2 in 1997, Virtua Striker 2 '99 on Model 3 (also in this app), Virtua Striker 3 in 2001 and Virtua Striker 4 in 2004. This app plays Revision A.
