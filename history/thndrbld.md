# Thunder Blade

Sega · 1987 · Sega X Board

Thunder Blade puts you at the controls of an attack helicopter. Each stage switches between a view from above, flying over city streets, and a view from behind the helicopter as it weaves between buildings, canyon walls and enemy tanks.

You fire guns and missiles from the stick and set your speed with a throttle. It came in upright and deluxe cabinets; this set is the upright one.

It was ported to the Master System and to several home computers.
