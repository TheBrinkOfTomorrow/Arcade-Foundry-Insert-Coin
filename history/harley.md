# Harley-Davidson & L.A. Riders

Sega AM1 · 1997 · Sega Model 3

Harley-Davidson & L.A. Riders sets you loose on a Harley across a recreated Los Angeles. Sega's AM1 team made it, and it was the first game on the Step 2 version of the Model 3 board. Harley-Davidson lent the team real motorcycles so each bike in the game would ride like the original.

It is an open-city driving game: you race to checkpoints at Los Angeles landmarks and along its freeways, picking your own way through traffic and taking shortcuts and bonus items for extra time and points.

The deluxe cabinet had a 50-inch projection screen and a tilting seat, and both versions had a subwoofer in the seat; up to four cabinets could be linked. It never came to home consoles.
