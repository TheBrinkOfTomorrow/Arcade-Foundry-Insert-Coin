# Racing Hero

Sega · 1989 · Sega X Board

Racing Hero is a motorbike racing game. You ride a race bike against the clock past beaches, palm trees and mountains, overtaking cars and rival bikes to reach each checkpoint before time runs out. You steer with a motorbike handlebar, with a throttle grip and a brake.

It ran on the X Board, the hardware behind After Burner and Thunder Blade. Its main program is protected by a Hitachi FD1094 encrypted CPU.
