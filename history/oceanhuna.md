# The Ocean Hunter

Sega · 1998 · Sega Model 3

The Ocean Hunter is an undersea light-gun game set in a steampunk world, where giant sea monsters are sinking ships and attacking harbours. Bounties are on their heads, and two underwater hunters, Torel and Chris, set out to collect them. The cabinet's two guns are mounted turrets.

The game moves you through sunken ships, underwater temples and ruined cities, shooting hostile sea life, with mini-bosses along the way and a legendary monster at the end of each sea: the Kraken, the Leviathan, Charybdis, Karkinos and Midgardsorm among them. The final boss, Rahab, turns out to have made the others to protect the oceans from pollution.

It never came to home consoles.
