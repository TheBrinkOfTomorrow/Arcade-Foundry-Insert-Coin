# Last Bronx

Sega AM3 · 1996 · Sega Model 2B

Last Bronx is a 3D fighting game with weapons, set in an alternative Tokyo after Japan's economic bubble has burst and street gangs fight over the city. Sega's AM3 team made it, and it borrows Virtua Fighter's three-button layout of punch (here the weapon), kick and guard.

Each of the nine fighters carries a weapon: Yusaku's three-section staff, Joe's nunchaku, Zaimoku's hammer, Kurosawa's wooden sword, Nagi's sai, Yoko's tonfa, Tommy's bo staff and Lisa's double sticks. Fast swings leave trails of afterimages so you can follow them, and rounds last only thirty seconds, so fights are quick and aggressive.

It was a hit in Japan, less so in the West, and became a small franchise there with a novel, manga, a radio drama and a live-action film. It came to the Saturn in 1997 with a new story mode, to Windows in 1998 and to the PlayStation 2 in Japan in 2006. This app plays the Export version, Revision A.
