# Galaxy Force 2

Sega · 1988 · Sega Y Board

Galaxy Force 2 is a space combat shooter seen from behind the player's fighter, the TRY-Z. The player flies through planets, fortresses and asteroid fields of the Fourth Empire, shooting down enemy craft with lasers and lock-on missiles while watching the energy gauge, which runs down with time and with hits, and steering the throttle to speed up through tunnels or slow down to aim.

It ran on Sega's Y Board, which drives its sprites with three 68000 processors and can rotate the whole sprite layer; the deluxe cabinet was a moving cockpit that rolled and pitched with the ship.
