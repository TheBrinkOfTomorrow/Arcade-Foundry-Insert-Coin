# Star Wars Trilogy Arcade

Sega AM Annex · 1998 · Sega Model 3

Star Wars Trilogy Arcade takes you through the big moments of all three original Star Wars films, set to John Williams' music. It was made by Sega AM Annex, the team behind Sega Rally, with Kenji Sasaki among its directors.

Each film is a mission. On Hoth you shoot down AT-STs and probe droids, help the snowspeeders trip the AT-ATs and escape through the rebel base. On Endor you chase scout troopers on a speeder bike, and over the Death Star you fly the X-wing battle and the trench run. Finish the missions and you take up a lightsaber: first to deflect Boba Fett's blaster shots into the Sarlacc pit, then to duel Darth Vader.

It was Sega's second Star Wars arcade game, after Star Wars Arcade on Model 1, also in this app.
