# Cyber Troopers Virtual-On

Sega AM3 · 1995 · Sega Model 2B

Cyber Troopers Virtual-On is a one-on-one battle between giant robots, the Virtuaroids, played with two joysticks. Sega's AM3 team made it, produced by Juro Watari. Each stick has a trigger and a button: push both sticks forward to walk, apart to jump, against each other to turn, and fire the weapon in each hand or pull both triggers for the centre one.

Battles take place in 3D arenas where you dash, jump and strafe around your opponent, firing from cover. The robots, among them Temjin and Raiden, have the clean, distinctive designs that made the series a favourite with mecha fans.

It was released in early 1996 and came to the Saturn the same year, with online play through NetLink and XBAND, and later to Windows, the PlayStation 2 and the PlayStation 4. The series continued with Virtual-On Oratorio Tangram on Model 3 in 1998, also in this app. This app plays the Twin cabinet, Export version.
