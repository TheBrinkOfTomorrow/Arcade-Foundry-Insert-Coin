# The House of the Dead 4

Sega · 2005 · Sega Lindbergh

The House of the Dead 4 was one of the first games on Sega's Lindbergh board, a PC-based arcade system, and it showed it off with crowds of zombies on screen at once. Takashi Oda, the director of the first House of the Dead, directed it. It is set in 2003, between The House of the Dead 2 and III: James Taylor, back from the second game, and new agent Kate Green are trapped underground by an earthquake and uncover a plan to launch nuclear missiles around the world.

The pistols of the earlier games give way to submachine guns that fire as long as you hold the trigger. You reload by shaking the gun or pointing it away, shake it hard to break free when a creature grabs you, and throw grenades to clear a crowd. There are branching paths and four endings.

IGN called it possibly the most visually impressive arcade game ever made. A ride version, The House of the Dead 4 Special, added moving seats and huge screens, and the game came to the PlayStation 3 in 2012 with PlayStation Move support. This app plays Revision A.
