# Sky Target

Sega · 1995 · Sega Model 2A

Sky Target is a jet-fighter shooting game that plays much like a 3D After Burner. Sega never called it a sequel, but it was compared to After Burner from the start, and it includes a new arrangement of After Burner's theme music.

You fly an F-14D Super Tomcat by default, or a Rafale M, an F-16C or an F-15 S/MTD, with an analog flight stick and a camera behind the plane. Sweep the sight over enemies to lock on and fire homing missiles. Some stages let you choose between two routes, and each ends with a large, science-fiction boss to destroy against the clock.

It came to the Saturn in 1997, ported by Appaloosa Interactive with a new ranking mode, and to Windows in Japan the same year.
