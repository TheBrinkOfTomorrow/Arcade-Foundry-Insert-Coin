# Virtua Fighter 3

Sega AM2 · 1996 · Sega Model 3

Virtua Fighter 3 launched Sega's Model 3 board, and it was the first game released on it. Yu Suzuki produced it and co-directed it with Daichi Katagiri, and its detailed fighters and stages were a showcase for what the new hardware could draw.

It adds a fourth button, Dodge, which steps into or out of the screen to slip past an attack and set up a counter. The ground is no longer flat: fights take place on the steps of the Great Wall, a sloping rooftop and a raft bobbing on the water, and the terrain changes how moves land. Two new fighters join the cast: Aoi Umenokoji, an aiki-jujutsu expert, and Taka-Arashi, a sumo wrestler.

About 30,000 cabinets were sold by 1997. A Saturn version was announced but never released; the updated Virtua Fighter 3tb, which added team battles, came to the Dreamcast in 1998.
