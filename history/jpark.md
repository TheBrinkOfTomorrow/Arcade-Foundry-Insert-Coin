# Jurassic Park

Sega · 1993 · Sega System 32

Jurassic Park is a shooting game based on the 1993 film. A few months after the events of the film, two players return to the island in a jeep, each with a gun mounted in front of them, and fight their way past the park's escaped dinosaurs, from raptors in the jungle to the Tyrannosaurus that chases the jeep.

The deluxe cabinet is a ride: the two players sit in a moving seat that a separate drive board tilts and shakes along with the action. That drive board is not emulated here, as in MAME, so the game runs as a standard cabinet.
