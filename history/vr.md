# Virtua Racing

Sega AM2 · 1992 · Sega Model 1

Virtua Racing was the first game on Sega's Model 1 board and the game that brought fast, fully 3D polygon racing to arcades. Yu Suzuki's AM2 team made it to prove the board could draw a full racing world in real time, and the flat-shaded cars and tracks looked unlike anything else in arcades in 1992.

You race open-wheel Formula cars on three circuits: Big Forest, Bay Bridge and Acropolis. Its best-known idea is the V.R. View system: four buttons switch the camera between the cockpit, chase and distant views at any moment, something racing games have copied ever since. Cabinets could be linked for multiplayer races, and a deluxe version, Virtua Formula, ran in Sega's large entertainment centres.

Virtua Racing laid the ground for the 3D games that followed on Sega's boards, Virtua Fighter and Daytona USA among them. It was brought home on the Mega Drive with an extra processor chip inside the cartridge, then on the 32X and Saturn, and decades later as a faithful Nintendo Switch release.
