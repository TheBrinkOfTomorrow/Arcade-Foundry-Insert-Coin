# Out Run

Sega · 1986 · Sega Out Run board

Out Run is a driving game about the drive itself rather than a race: you take a red open-top Ferrari Testarossa, with a passenger beside you, across a string of sunny stages against the clock. It was made by Yu Suzuki's team, and the deluxe sit-down cabinet moved with the car as you steered.

At the end of each stage the road forks, and you pick left or right. The stages form a pyramid, so every run passes through five of the fifteen and ends at one of five goals, each with its own ending. Before you start you choose the music on the car radio, from tracks by Hiroshi Kawaguchi that became some of the best-known in arcade history.

It was one of Sega's biggest arcade hits of the 1980s and was ported to many home computers and consoles.
