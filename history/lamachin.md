# L.A. Machineguns

Sega · 1998 · Sega Model 3

L.A. Machineguns: Rage of the Machines is the sequel to Gunblade NY, developed under the working title Gunblade 2. It is set twenty years after the first game: this time the robots have come to Los Angeles, and two agents ride hoverbikes through the city, firing mounted machine guns.

As in Gunblade NY, you fire mounted guns while the game carries you along. There are many more flying enemies than before, and two players can play together.

Both games came to the Wii together in 2010 in Sega Arcade Hits Pack. Gunblade NY is in this app too.
