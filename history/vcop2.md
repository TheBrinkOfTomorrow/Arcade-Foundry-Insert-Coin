# Virtua Cop 2

Sega AM2 · 1995 · Sega Model 2

Virtua Cop 2 is AM2's sequel to Virtua Cop, made a year later under producer Yu Suzuki and director Wataru Kawashima. Rage and Smarty are back, joined by a new partner, Janet Marshall, against the E.V.I.L. syndicate.

It builds on the first game in every direction. At points you choose between two routes, there are car chases, every level ends with a boss, and shooting objects in the background reveals power-ups. The "justice shot", disarming a criminal instead of killing them, still scores extra points, and an unbroken run of hits raises a score multiplier.

It was the highest-earning dedicated arcade game in Japan in 1996. It came to the Saturn in 1996, Windows in 1997, the Dreamcast in 2000 and the PlayStation 2 in 2002, and it was followed by Virtua Cop 3 in 2003.
