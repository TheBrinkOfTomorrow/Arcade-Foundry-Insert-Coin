# Hang-On

Sega · 1985 · Sega Hang-On board

Hang-On is Sega's 1985 motorbike racer and the first of Yu Suzuki's "taikan" (body sensation) games. You race a bike against the clock through five stages, passing other riders and reaching each checkpoint before time runs out.

The deluxe cabinet was a full-size motorbike: you leaned it left and right to steer, with a twist grip for the throttle and a lever for the brake. Its sprite-scaling hardware led straight to Space Harrier and Out Run, and to the 1987 sequel, Super Hang-On.

It was ported to the Sega Master System.
