# The Lost World

Sega AM3 · 1997 · Sega Model 3

The Lost World: Jurassic Park is a light-gun game based on the film, made by Sega's AM3 team and directed by Shinichi Ogasawara. It was the first shooting game on the Model 3 board, chosen because Model 2 could not do what the team wanted.

Ian Malcolm and Sarah Harding have gone missing on Isla Sorna, and you play one of two rangers sent to find them, armed with tranquilliser guns. Velociraptors, Compsognathus, Dilophosaurus and pterosaurs attack along the way, and four of the five levels end with a boss: a Carnotaurus, a Deinosuchus and the Tyrannosaurus among them. Rescuing people earns better weapons or health.

The standard cabinet seats two players in front of a 50-inch screen with surround sound. GamePro called its graphics a giant leap forward for gun games. A Dreamcast version was announced but cancelled, so it stayed in arcades.
