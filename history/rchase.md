# Rail Chase

Sega · 1991 · Sega Y Board

Rail Chase is a two-player shooting game set on a runaway mine cart. Following the pursuit of a stolen treasure, the players ride the cart through caves, snowfields, jungles and waterfalls, shooting monsters and the villain's henchmen with guns mounted on the cart, while the route forks and drops ahead of them.

It ran on Sega's Y Board, three 68000 processors and a sprite layer that can rotate and scale as a whole. The deluxe cabinet was a moving two-seat cart that tilted and jolted with the ride; Sega followed it with Rail Chase 2 on Model 2.
