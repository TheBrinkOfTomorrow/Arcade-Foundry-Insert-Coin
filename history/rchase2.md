# Rail Chase 2

Sega AM3 · 1994 · Sega Model 2B

Rail Chase 2 is a mine-cart ride with guns. Made by Sega's AM3 team under producer Hisao Oguchi, it follows Rail Chase of 1991, which used flat sprites on the System 32 board; the sequel rebuilt the ride in texture-mapped 3D on Model 2. Sega also ran a Rail Chase ride at its Joypolis theme park in Yokohama.

You ride a cart down the tracks of a mine, through its caves and tunnels, shooting enemies from a mounted gun while the cabinet moves with the ride. You choose your route by shooting the signs at the junctions. Two players can ride together.

It never came to home consoles. This app plays Revision A.
