# Golden Axe: The Revenge of Death Adder

Sega · 1992 · Sega System 32

Golden Axe: The Revenge of Death Adder is the arcade sequel to Golden Axe, a side-scrolling fighting game for up to four players. Each picks one of four heroes, among them the barbarian Stern and the centaur Dora, and fights through the land of Yuria with swords, axes and magic to defeat the returned Death Adder.

The game ran on Sega's System 32 board with a separate NEC V25 processor guarding it as copy protection; that chip is emulated here too. The arcade cabinet takes four players; here up to two can play.
