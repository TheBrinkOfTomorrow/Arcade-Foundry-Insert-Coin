# Virtua Cop 3

Sega AM2 · 2003 · Sega Chihiro

Virtua Cop 3 brought back Sega AM2's police light-gun series after eight years, on the Chihiro board. Michael "Rage" Hardy, James "Smarty" Cools and Janet Marshall of the Virtua City Police take on a crime syndicate across three missions: a pharmacy, a bank robbery that leads into the subway, and a military base. The missions can be played in any order, and a hidden final mission waits for players who do well enough.

The new trick is a foot pedal on the cabinet: stepping on it slows time while a meter lasts, so a player can pick off several enemies before they react, and shooting enemies fills the meter again. The gun has a button to switch to the weapons picked up along the way.

The game came in standard and deluxe cabinets. An Xbox version was planned but cancelled, reportedly because of the cost of a light gun for that console, so Virtua Cop 3 never came home.
