# Strike Fighter

Sega · 1991 · Sega Y Board

Strike Fighter is a jet combat game built on G-LOC: Air Battle's engine, sharing much of its graphics and sound. The player flies a run of missions against enemy fighters, bombers and ground targets with a vulcan cannon, missiles and an after burner, viewing the action from the cockpit or from behind the plane.

Like G-LOC it ran on Sega's Y Board, three 68000 processors and a sprite layer that can rotate as a whole, which turns the horizon as the plane banks.
