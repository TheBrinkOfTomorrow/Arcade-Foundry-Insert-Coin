# Sega Rally 2

Sega AM Annex · 1998 · Sega Model 3

Sega Rally 2 is the Model 3 sequel to Sega Rally Championship, made by the same core team, AM Annex, with Kenji Sasaki directing, Tetsuya Mizuguchi producing and Sohei Yamamoto programming. AM4 designed the cabinet.

The Celica, the Delta and the hidden Stratos return, joined by newer cars from Toyota, Lancia, Mitsubishi, Subaru, Fiat, Peugeot, Renault and Ford. New stages take you onto snow and around an island, and an updated version of the first game's desert course is there too. The handling still changes with the surface under each wheel, and the cabinet now has a hand brake for tight corners.

It came to the Dreamcast in 1999 with a ten-year championship mode, though at half the arcade's frame rate, and to Windows. The series continued with Sega Rally 2006 and Sega Rally Revo.
