# The House of the Dead

Sega AM1 · 1997 · Sega Model 2C

The House of the Dead is the zombie light-gun game that started a long series. Sega's AM1 team made it on the Virtua Cop engine, directed by Takashi Oda and produced by Rikiya Nakagawa. Dr. Curien's experiments have filled his mansion with monsters, and AMS agents Thomas Rogan and "G" go in to stop him and save Rogan's fiancée, Sophie.

Reload by shooting off the screen, rescue hostages from the creatures to win back health, and take care not to shoot them yourself. The route branches according to what you do: save the hostage on the bridge at the start and you go in through the front door, fail and you go through the sewers. Bosses with tarot-card names, the Chariot, the Hanged Man, the Hermit and the Magician, wait at the end of each chapter.

Alongside Resident Evil, it helped bring zombies back into games and wider pop culture. It came to the Saturn and Windows in 1998, led to The House of the Dead 2, a 2003 film and a 2022 remake, and The House of the Dead 4 is in this app too.
