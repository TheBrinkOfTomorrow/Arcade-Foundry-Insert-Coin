# Wing War

Sega · 1994 · Sega Model 1

Wing War is a head-to-head air battle on Sega's Model 1 board: two players, each in their own plane, try to shoot the other down. It came in a two-seat sit-down cabinet and in Sega's R360, a cockpit that spins a full circle in any direction, and it was only the second game ever made for the R360.

There are two ways to fly. Dogfight mode is fast and arcade-like; Expert mode makes the planes handle more like real aircraft and adds a throttle. You fly with a yoke, firing machine guns and homing missiles with two triggers, and four buttons switch the view.

A home version for the 32X was started but cancelled, so it stayed in arcades.
