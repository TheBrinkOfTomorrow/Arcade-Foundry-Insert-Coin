# Wave Runner

Sega · 1996 · Sega Model 2C

Wave Runner is a personal watercraft race, played on a cabinet shaped like a Yamaha WaveRunner, the machine Sega based the game on with Yamaha's help. You steer with the handlebars or by leaning your weight into the turns, as on the real thing.

There are three courses, Novice, Intermediate and Expert, with ramps for jumps and tricks along the way. Up to four cabinets can be linked for one race.

The game never came to home consoles. Sega followed it with Wave Runner GP on its NAOMI board in 2001. This app plays the Japanese version, Revision A.
