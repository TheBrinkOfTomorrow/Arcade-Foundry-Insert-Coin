# Sonic Championship

Sega AM2 · 1996 · Sega Model 2B

Sonic Championship, called Sonic the Fighters in Japan, was the first 3D game in the Sonic series and its first fighting game. It started when Yu Suzuki saw an AM2 artist testing a 3D Sonic model in the Fighting Vipers engine; Sonic Team's Yuji Naka approved the idea and sent the team a Sonic figure to work from. Hiroshi Kataoka directed it.

Eight characters fight in a simple, beginner-friendly game: Sonic, Tails, Knuckles, Amy, Espio, Fang, Bean and Bark. The small arms of Sonic's cast made normal blocking hard to read, so each fighter has a limited number of barriers instead, and rings spill out to show damage.

A Saturn version was planned but never released, so for nearly a decade the game was arcade only. It finally came home in Sonic Gems Collection in 2005, on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in 2012 with online play, and as a playable arcade machine in Lost Judgment and Like a Dragon Gaiden. This app plays the USA version.
