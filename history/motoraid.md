# Motor Raid

Sega AM1 · 1997 · Sega Model 2A

Motor Raid is a futuristic motorcycle race where hitting your rivals is part of the plan. Made by Sega's AM1 team, it was sold as a conversion kit for Manx TT Superbike cabinets, so you twist the handle to accelerate and lean the bike to steer, as on Manx TT, but you also swing weapons to knock other riders down.

You choose one of four riders, Geila, Gunz, Io and Robin, and race across alien planets with names like Yendas, Reef 8 and Junos. A turbo gauge fills as you ride and gives three levels of boost; the strongest can knock out anyone in your way. A secret planet, Segal, has a giant Sonic statue beside the track. Cabinets can be linked for four-player battles.

Motor Raid was never released for home consoles. For twenty years the only way to play it was in an arcade, until it appeared as a playable machine inside Sega's own Judgment in 2018 and Lost Judgment in 2021.
