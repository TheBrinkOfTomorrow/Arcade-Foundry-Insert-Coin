# Rad Rally

Sega · 1991 · Sega System 32

Rad Rally is a rally game from Sega's System 32 board, seen from the driver's seat: the car's bonnet and dashboard fill the bottom of the screen, and a rear-view mirror across the top shows the road behind you.

You race a field of rival cars from start to goal on each course, against the clock and for a finishing rank, with a two-position gear lever and the board's scaling sprites for the scenery rushing past.
