# Virtua Striker 2 '99

Sega AM2 · 1999 · Sega Model 3

Virtua Striker 2 moved AM2's football game to the Model 3 board, with more detailed players and stadiums than the first game. Like other AM2 arcade games, it was updated year by year with new teams and tweaks, and Version '99 is one of those updates.

It keeps the first game's three-button controls, pass, long ball and shoot, so anyone can pick it up, and two players can play against each other.

The Dreamcast version of 1999 and 2000, made by Genki, brought home the later Version 2000.1. The series continued with Virtua Striker 3 in 2001 and Virtua Striker 4 in 2004.
