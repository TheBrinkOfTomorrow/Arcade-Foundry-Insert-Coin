# Zero Gunner

Psikyo · 1997 · Sega Model 2B

Zero Gunner is a sideways-scrolling shooting game from Psikyo, a studio famous for its 2D shooters. Built for Sega's Model 2 board, it keeps the classic shooter layout but draws its stages and bosses in 3D.

You fly a Cobra, an Apache or a heavy gunship, each with a normal shot and a powerful sound wave that sweeps the screen. Its special idea is the lock-on: fix your sights on an enemy and you keep firing at it while you fly freely around it, even circling behind it. Two players can play together.

Psikyo followed it with Zero Gunner 2 on Sega's NAOMI board and the Dreamcast. This app plays the Export version.
