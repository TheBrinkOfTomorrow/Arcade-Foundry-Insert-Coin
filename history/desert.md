# Desert Tank

Martin Marietta and Sega · 1994 · Sega Model 2

Desert Tank came straight out of the military simulator business. The 3D graphics technology in Sega's Model 2 board came from GE Aerospace's simulators, and engineers from that side of the deal, by then part of Martin Marietta, built this game with Sega. It was one of the very first Model 2 games, following Daytona USA, and the first game from the team that later became Real 3D.

You command a tank on timed missions across an open 3D desert, driving with a yoke, a gear stick and an accelerator pedal and firing the main cannon and machine guns. You can switch between a view from the turret, one from behind the tank and one from overhead.

It came in a large single-seat deluxe cabinet, so it was mostly seen in big arcades, and it never came to home consoles. It is one of the rarer and less-known Model 2 games.
