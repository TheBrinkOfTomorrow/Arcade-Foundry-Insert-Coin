# Turbo Out Run

Sega · 1989 · Sega Out Run board

Turbo Out Run is the follow-up to Out Run, sold as an upgrade kit for the original cabinets. This time it is a race: you drive the red Ferrari across the United States, from New York to Los Angeles, trying to reach each checkpoint ahead of a rival car and the clock.

A turbo button gives a burst of speed until the engine overheats, and between stages you can tune the car with better grip, a stronger engine or a faster turbo. Police cars join the traffic, and the stages run through changing weather and times of day.

It was ported to the Mega Drive and many home computers.
