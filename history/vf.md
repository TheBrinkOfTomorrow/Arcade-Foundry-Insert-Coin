# Virtua Fighter

Sega AM2 · 1993 · Sega Model 1

Virtua Fighter was the first 3D fighting game to make a real mark, and the second Model 1 game after Virtua Racing. Yu Suzuki's AM2 team built fighters out of polygons and animated them with careful attention to real martial arts, so moves had weight and timing instead of the special-move fireballs of the 2D fighting games of the time.

The controls are deliberately simple: a stick and three buttons, Guard, Punch and Kick. Eight fighters, among them Akira Yuki, Pai Chan, Jacky and Sarah Bryant, Wolf Hawkfield and Kage-Maru, face each other on a raised ring, and a fight can be won by knocking the opponent off the edge. The final opponent is Dural, a silver fighter who borrows everyone's moves.

The game started the whole 3D fighting genre and inspired series such as Tekken. Its Saturn version was a launch game in Japan in 1994 and helped sell the console. Virtua Fighter 2 followed on Model 2 in 1994 and Virtua Fighter 3 on Model 3 in 1996, and both are in this app too.
