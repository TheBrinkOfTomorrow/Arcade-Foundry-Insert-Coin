# Get Bass

Sega AM1 · 1998 · Sega Model 3

Get Bass is the Japanese original of Sega Bass Fishing. Its cabinet has a fishing rod with a working reel, its line running into the machine, which pulls back as the fish fights; you cast with the stick, work the lure and reel the fish in.

There are four stages at different times of day. Catch a target weight of bass before time runs out to move on; the last stage allows a single catch, from the biggest fish in the game. Each catch is weighed and ranked, from Small One to Huge.

It came in Standard, Upright and Deluxe cabinets, and this app has all three Japanese versions. The game became Sega Bass Fishing outside Japan, with home versions on the Dreamcast, Windows, the Wii, the Xbox 360, PlayStation 3 and Steam.
