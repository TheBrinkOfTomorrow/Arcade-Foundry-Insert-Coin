# Crazy Taxi High Roller

Hitmaker · 2003 · Sega Chihiro

Crazy Taxi High Roller is the arcade version of Crazy Taxi 3: High Roller, the third game in Hitmaker's series about picking up fares and getting them across town as fast and as wildly as possible. It came out on the Xbox in 2002, then in arcades in 2003 on the Chihiro board, which is built from the same parts as the Xbox.

Alongside the West Coast city of the first game and Small Apple from Crazy Taxi 2, now driven at night, there is a new city, Glitter Oasis, a desert resort in the style of Las Vegas. Each driver has their own car, with its own speed and handling. Drifts, jumps and near misses still earn extra money, and they can be chained into combos.

A Windows version followed in 2004.
